ALT: Two counts against how deeply a JSON document may nest, on a log y axis. The number of states the machine can be in doubles with every level, from 27 to 1,577, because the stack is a sequence of objects and arrays. The number of distinct masks those states need is 27 and stays 27 at every depth, because what may come next depends only on the innermost open container and never on the ones beneath it. That is why the whole table packs into 128 bytes and why the work per token at serving time is a lookup rather than a walk over the grammar.
