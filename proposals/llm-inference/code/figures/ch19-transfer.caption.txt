Computing a 8,192-token prefill takes 159 milliseconds; moving the 1,074 MB of keys and values it produced takes 1.2 milliseconds over NVLink inside a node, 21 over InfiniBand, and 344 over 25 gigabit Ethernet -- longer than the prefill itself. The Ethernet lines cross above the prefill line, which is where the move becomes the bottleneck; NVLink stays below one decode step at every length.
