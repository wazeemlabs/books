ALT: Bit layouts drawn to scale. Every format is a sign bit, then exponent bits, then mantissa bits. float16 and bfloat16 are both sixteen bits and divide them differently: float16 keeps 5 for the exponent and 10 for the mantissa, bfloat16 keeps 8 and 7. The exponent sets how large a number can be -- 65,504 against 3.39e+38 -- and the mantissa sets how many digits survive. bfloat16 is float32's exponent with most of the mantissa thrown away.
