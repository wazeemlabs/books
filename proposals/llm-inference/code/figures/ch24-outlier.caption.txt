ALT: Error on the ordinary weights, against the size of a single outlier placed among them, on log axes. With one scale for the whole tensor the error rises from 4.13e-02 to 2.48e-01 -- the ordinary weights are destroyed, because the grid has been stretched to reach a value none of them is near. With a scale per group of 32 the error barely moves, from 2.43e-02 to 2.61e-02, because the outlier stretches only its own group's grid. This is the argument for group-wise quantization, and it is why the group size is the number that matters.
