## The sample chapter

Chapter 12, "The KV Cache", is written to demonstrate that the
standards are achievable rather than aspirational. Every number in it
was measured on this machine; no number was typed into the manuscript
by hand.

```
cd code
make ch12      # re-measure, then rebuild figures, tables and the chapter
make check     # fail if the chapter has drifted from the measurements
```

`make check` is what CI runs. A sentence that quotes a measurement and
the measurement itself cannot be separated: prose numbers are
`{{placeholders}}` resolved from `results/ch12.json` by `bench/values.py`,
tables are inserted from `tables/`, and figures are regenerated by
`bench/figures.py` with their provenance written beside them.

Tier 0: it runs on a CPU in about four minutes and needs only NumPy and
Matplotlib. `environment.lock` pins what produced the committed numbers.

## What the sample chapter establishes

- The engine spine works as a teaching device: `tinyserve` grows from
  the naive loop (Ch 11) to a KV cache (Ch 12) in about 250 lines, and
  the gain is measurable and large.
- The cache is verified *exact* — `check_equivalence` asserts the two
  paths emit identical tokens — before any speedup is claimed.
- The measurements support the book's thesis on a laptop CPU: the
  cached path does 181x fewer multiply-adds but runs 69x faster,
  because decode stops being limited by arithmetic and starts being
  limited by memory traffic.
- Honesty survives contact with a noisy machine: the headline speedup
  moves ±17% across repeats on a shared vCPU, so the chapter reports it
  as "about 42x" and tells the reader which findings are stable.

## Open decisions

1. **Repositories.** These files should move to `llm-inference-book`
   (manuscript) and `llm-inference-code` (engine and harness), per the
   series pattern, leaving only the companion page in `books`.
2. **Manuscript tooling.** `bench/manuscript.py` is a placeholder for
   whatever `llm-course` already uses; match that instead of keeping
   two toolchains.
3. **The 8B-class running model.** Chosen by criteria at Chapter 10;
   see FACTS.md. The current Qwen generation ships no 8B, so this needs
   a fresh check at writing time.
4. **GPU budget.** Tiers 2–3 need an H100 for a few hours per part.
